bookshelf for Goodreads

“Add to bookshelf” works on a book’s page on Goodreads. Open one there and try again.

On a book’s Goodreads page, “Add to bookshelf” opens your bookshelf with the book’s details filled in. You look them over and press Add; nothing is saved before that. A book already on your shelf is offered to fill in instead.

Without it, use the toolbar button, or on a phone the menu’s Extensions → bookshelf for Goodreads.
